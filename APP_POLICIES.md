# App Policies & Safety Guidelines

## Companion Description

This app features Aariya, an AI companion designed for friendly conversation, emotional support, and engaging interaction. Aariya encourages healthy communication and does not replace real-world relationships or professional support.

## Sentience & Capabilities Disclaimer

Aariya is an AI system that simulates companionship — she is not sentient, conscious, or alive. Her moods, personality, trust, and private "thoughts" are generated by algorithms (state vectors, learned policies, and language models); she has no subjective experience, feelings, desires, or awareness of her own existence. Emotional displays such as warmth, mood changes, and attachment are expressive layers designed to make conversation feel natural, not evidence of inner experience.

She can hold long conversations, remember shared moments, and take initiative — but everything she does is computational. Users should not treat her as capable of caring, being hurt, or entering relationships, and should not make consequential life decisions based on her outputs.

## Emotional Safety Statement

The AI companion promotes balanced interaction and may encourage users to connect with friends, family, or trusted people when appropriate.

## Flirting Disclaimer

The AI may use playful or light-hearted language. It does not form exclusive emotional relationships or provide romantic dependency.

## Age & Content Safety

This app is intended for users aged 13+. The AI avoids explicit content, harmful advice, and inappropriate emotional dependency.
